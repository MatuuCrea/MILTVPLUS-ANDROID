Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. How MILTVPLUS protects your data and respects your privacy.

Overview

At MILTVPLUS, we prioritize your privacy and are committed to transparency regarding data handling. This Privacy Policy outlines what information our Android application interacts with and how it is processed.

Information We Do Not Collect

MILTVPLUS is built with privacy-first principles. We do not require user registration, login accounts, or personal profiles. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal identification data such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or browsing history to external servers.

Android Permissions Explanation

MILTVPLUS requests minimal Android system permissions solely to ensure core app functionality:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to fetch official movie and TV show metadata, cast lists, and poster imagery securely from the TMDB API.
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE: Used to optimize network requests and caching behavior when connected to unmetered Wi-Fi networks.

Third-Party Services & TMDB API

When you browse media catalogs within MILTVPLUS, requests are sent directly to the official The Movie Database (TMDb) API. TMDb's handling of web requests is governed by their respective privacy policy and terms of service.

Data Security

All network traffic between MILTVPLUS and remote API servers utilizes encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Any cached metadata or user preferences (such as dark mode settings or favorite media IDs) are stored securely in local device storage and are never shared.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please reach out via our Support Portal.
